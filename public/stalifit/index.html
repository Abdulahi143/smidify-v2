Account & data

Open Profile → Account to log out or request account deletion. Deleting StaliFit data does not delete source records in Apple Health.

Connected devices

Manage connections in Profile → My devices. Check that your watch has synced with its companion app and that Apple Health contains the readings you want to share.

When contacting support, include your app version and a description of the issue. Please leave passwords, verification codes and private health records out of your message.
