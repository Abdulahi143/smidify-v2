StaliFit privacy policy

Prepared 2 November 2026. Contact: hello@smidify.com.

Information used

StaliFit uses your account identity (including your email address or Apple relay address) to sign you in. If you enter them, your profile, measurements, goals, food diary, custom foods, nutrition targets, workout records, saved routines and weekly plans are stored on your device and in your account's Supabase storage to support saving and restoration across devices.

Health and connected devices

With your permission, StaliFit reads Apple Health activity, sleep, weight, workouts and selected heart/oxygen readings. Those readings are processed on your device and are not included in the StaliFit account backup. Progress views can combine them with your logged records on the device. Direct supported-watch readings and pairing information also remain on your device.

If you connect Withings, the server stores imported weight readings and encrypted connection credentials so it can sync your scale. Disconnect in My devices to stop that connection. Removing a StaliFit connection does not erase source data at Apple or Withings.

Other services

Online product searches and barcode lookups send the search text or barcode to Open Food Facts. Account authentication and saved records are processed by Supabase. Sign in with Apple uses Apple authentication. If a local AI model is installed, AI processing runs on your device; assistant conversation is held in memory. Optional reminders use your phone's notification system.

StaliFit does not sell personal data, use health data for advertising, or include advertising tracking.

Your choices

You can edit your records in the app, manage device connections under My devices, and manage Apple Health access through Apple's Health settings. Logging out retains saved account records. Account → Delete account removes your StaliFit account, associated server records and this device's account cache; source records in Apple Health and device-provider accounts remain. Apple sign-in access is revoked during Apple-account deletion after confirmation.

Other devices may retain previously downloaded records until their local session and cache are cleared. Provider-managed infrastructure backups and necessary operational logs may remain for the providers' retention periods. Contact hello@smidify.com with privacy or deletion questions.

Security and changes

Access to saved account records is restricted to the authenticated account. Provider credentials stay on the server or in the device's protected key storage. No system can guarantee absolute security.

We will update this policy when the app's data practices change.
